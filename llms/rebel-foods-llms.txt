# Rebel Foods

> Rebel Foods is an Indian cloud-kitchen operator headquartered in Mumbai that describes itself as the
> world's largest chain of internet restaurants. It runs delivery-first food brands out of a shared
> network of cloud kitchens and consolidates them for consumers in the EatSure ordering app.

**There is no public Rebel Foods API.** As of 2026-08-26 the company publishes no developer portal, no
API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, and no
`/.well-known/` documents. Do not attempt to call a Rebel Foods API; none is documented for third-party
use. This file was generated by API Evangelist from the company's public website, not published by
Rebel Foods.

## What the company operates

- Brands: Faasos, Behrouz Biryani, Oven Story, Firangi Bake, The Good Bowl, Sweet Truth, Mandarin Oak,
  The Biryani Life, Lunch Box
- EatSure: the consumer ordering app and website that aggregates those brands into a single order
- Rebel Operating System: the internal platform for kitchen workflow, inventory, demand forecasting,
  order routing and delivery logistics
- Rebel Launcher: the partner programme through which third-party restaurant chains run on Rebel Foods'
  kitchen network and platform — a commercial partnership, not a self-serve developer product
